Film Grain Studio — Curated Spectral Film LUT profiles

The five 33x33x33 SDR film negative/print LUTs in Profiles/ were generated
offline from Jan Lohse's Spectral Film LUT v0.18.13 source code and film data.
Upstream: https://github.com/JanLohse/spectral_film_lut
Revision: bcbcfc6861f49cc706dc5d00ea048e4853060aab
License: MIT (see LICENSE_SpectralFilmLUT.txt)

Regeneration: Tools/FilmLut/generate_profiles.py (developer tool, Python only).
These LUT files and the compact FGS C# adjustment code do not embed Python,
NumPy, SciPy, Numba, Qt, or any upstream executable.

INPUT: SDR ITU-R BT.709 signals only.
OUTPUT: Rec.709 gamut, display gamma 2.4.
HDR PQ/HLG signals must NOT be passed directly into these profiles.

FGS adjusts exposure and perceived warmth before the LUT, then saturation
and blend strength after the LUT. These four edits are creative transforms,
not a re-simulation of the upstream spectral model at new physical settings.
