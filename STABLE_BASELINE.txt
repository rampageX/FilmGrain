Film Grain Studio formal stable baseline

Baseline:
v4.5.1

Supersedes:
v4.5.0

Major changes:
- H.264 x264 Grain now defaults to Faster + tune grain + VBR single-pass for substantially lower day-to-day CPU encoding time.
- VBR 2-Pass remains available in Advanced > Encode for users who want more precise average-bitrate / file-size allocation.
- x264 Preset is selectable in Advanced > Encode: Faster (default/recommended), Medium, Slow.
- Main x264 Grain and AV1/HEVC H.264 upload copies share the same x264 Preset / Pass setting; upload copies remain High 8-bit.
- Automatic bitrate, High Motion, maxrate = average x3, bufsize = average x6, 10-bit preprocessing / 8-bit dither, and experimental High10 behavior are unchanged.

Validation:
The v4.5.1 pre-release build was user-tested successfully, including the new Faster + VBR single-pass path and related combinations, before this formal release.

Preserved baseline behavior:
All v4.5.0 AV1 / HEVC / x264 bitrate-policy behavior remains intact except for the user-selectable x264 Preset / Pass strategy. The standalone AV1_FilmGrain_Bake_for_Social_Upload.bat keeps its existing slow + tune grain + 2-pass behavior.
