Film Grain Studio formal stable baseline

Baseline:
v4.5.0

Supersedes:
v4.4.5

Major changes:
- H.264 x264 Grain is now a first-class main encoder alongside AV1 and HEVC.
- AV1 / HEVC / x264 share visible automatic bitrate recommendations based on codec, output resolution, final FPS and High Motion state; manual bitrate remains authoritative.
- VBV remains maxrate = average x3 and bufsize = average x6.
- High Motion also enables supported encoder motion optimizations without changing validated AQ strength.
- Experimental H.264 High10 is available in Advanced > Encode and is disabled by default.
- HEVC / AV1 H.264 upload copies are now fixed to x264 Grain 8-bit with the same new automatic/manual bitrate policy.

Validation:
HEVC, x264 Grain, High10, OpenSVPFlow combinations and all AV1-related paths were user-tested successfully before this formal release.

Preserved baseline behavior:
The v4.4.3 MPEG-TS OpenSVPFlow A/V timestamp synchronization fix and the v4.4.5 interpolation + H.264 upload-copy reuse logic remain unchanged.
