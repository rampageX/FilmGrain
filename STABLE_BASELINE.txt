v4.7.0 major additions:
- Digital Grain / Fast Noise is available across AV1, HEVC and x264 main lines.
- Digital Grain strength slider: 0.10-1.00, default 0.55.
- AV1 / HEVC HDR Preserve supports a 10-bit luma-only Digital Grain path.
- HDR handling offers Auto / Keep HDR / Force SDR.
- HDR-to-SDR Tone Mapping supports Hable (default), Mobius, Reinhard, Gamma, Linear and Clip.
- Auto mode Tone Maps HDR to BT.709 SDR when x264, BT.709 LUT, OpenSVPFlow or H.264 upload requires an SDR workflow.
- HDR-to-SDR uses a random-suffix 10-bit HEVC Main10 lossless working file and cleans it after the job.
- AV1 HDR Digital Grain and HDR-to-SDR + LUT were user-tested successfully before v4.7.0 packaging.

- Windows packaging compatibility rules and release validation.
- LUT Gallery Update Preview synchronization and safe stale-preview cleanup.
- HDR Preserve for AV1 Main10 / HEVC Main10.
- AV1 SFE, OpenSVPFlow updater, H.264 upload bitrate fix, Batch Summary and all prior stable functionality.
