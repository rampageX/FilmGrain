Baseline:
v4.7.5

Formal release:
FilmGrain_Studio_v4.7.5_Stable.zip

v4.7.5 major additions:
- Digital Grain, Grain Plate and GPU Film Grain (FGSIM) share the Film Grain Strength control.
- FGSIM retains its validated Light / Medium / Heavy preset mapping and unchanged shader/filter pipeline.
- HEVC + FGSIM adds optional Standard CQ27 / QP18-26 and High Quality CQ23 / QP18-24 entries to the video bitrate selector.
- Original automatic or manual VBR remains the default; CQ is an optional remedy when banding appears.
- FGSIM and BWDIF Vulkan reuse the same vk filter device, fixing the duplicate filter device failure.
- AV1, x264 Grain, normal HEVC VBR, Digital Grain, Grain Plate, containers, AAC 256k, interpolation, LUT and HDR workflows remain unchanged.

Inherited from v4.7.0:
- Digital Grain / Fast Noise across AV1, HEVC and x264 main lines.
- AV1 / HEVC HDR Preserve and HDR-to-SDR Tone Mapping fallback.
- Windows packaging compatibility rules and release validation.
- LUT Gallery, AV1 SFE, OpenSVPFlow updater, H.264 upload bitrate fix, Batch Summary and all prior stable functionality.
